    About this model

    A neural network with two hidden layers (64 and 32 units) trained with scikit-learn on the UCI Default of Credit Card Clients data: 30,000 card holders in Taiwan, April to September 2005 (Yeh & Lien, 2009). It is the configuration selected in my Module 8 assignment for AAI6610.

    At the usual 0.50 cutoff the network misses most defaulters, so this app flags anyone at 0.29 or above. That cutoff was chosen from cross-validated F1 on the training data only.

    This is a course project, not a lending tool, and it should not be used to make decisions about real people.
